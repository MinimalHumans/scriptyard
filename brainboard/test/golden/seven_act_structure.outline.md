# Seven Act Structure

## Act 1 — Act Seven : Climax & Resolution

> The protagonist makes a final push to resolve the conflict, leading to the climax. Afterward, the story concludes with a resolution that shows the outcome and lasting impact of the journey.

## Act 2 — Act One : The Hook

> This opening act grabs attention and introduces the protagonist in their normal world. It sets tone and genre while hinting at the central conflict to come.

## Act 3 — Act Two : The Inciting Incident

> A key event disrupts the protagonist’s life and presents a problem or opportunity. This is where the main story truly begins to take shape.

## Act 4 — Act Three : The First Plot Point

> The protagonist makes a decisive choice that commits them to the journey. They leave their comfort zone, and the stakes become clear.

## Act 5 — Act Four : Rising Action

> The protagonist actively pursues their goal, encountering obstacles, allies, and enemies. Tension builds steadily as complications arise.

## Act 6 — Act Five : Midpoint

> A major turning point shifts the story—often a revelation, reversal, or significant success/failure. It raises the stakes and changes the protagonist’s approach.

## Act 7 — Act Six : Crisis (or Low Point)

> Everything seems to fall apart. The protagonist faces their greatest setback or internal conflict, often questioning their ability to succeed.
